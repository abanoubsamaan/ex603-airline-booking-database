# Recommendations

We have two different problems here, so they need two different fixes. Flights 2 and 3 have bookings but a lot of no-shows. Flights 14 and 15 have never had a single booking.

## Flights that fail often (2 and 3)

About 6% of bookings across the platform (12 out of 200) end as a passenger no-show. Flight 2 is at about 19% (3 of 16 bookings) and flight 3 is at about 15% (2 of 13), so roughly 2.5 to 3 times higher than normal.

Other flights from the same countries have much lower no-show rates and similar numbers of bookings, so it's not just that these flights are busy or leave from Japan or Korea. Both also share Changi (SIN) as a route, but SIN is linked to 5 of the 12 flights with routes, so that could be a coincidence.

What I'd do:
- Look at the passengers and bookings on flights 2 and 3 to see if there's a pattern, like booking country, fare, or how early people booked.
- If there's a pattern, try a simple fix on just these two flights, like a reminder before departure or a small cancellation fee.
- Watch them for a few months before changing anything for the whole platform.


## Flights that never started (14 and 15)

Flights 14 and 15 have no bookings at all. Both are active and have been scheduled since 2022, so they're not new or retired. They never showed up in my earlier reports because those only included flights with bookings.

What I'd do:
- Ask operations if these flights are really open for sale and check how they're listed and priced.
- If they're not supposed to operate, mark them inactive.
- If they should be selling, fix the listing or the data problem.
- Update our regular reports to include flights with zero bookings so this doesn't stay hidden.

The data doesn't tell us why they have no bookings. It could be a setup problem, missing data, or no demand, so someone has to check.

## Why different fixes

Flights 2 and 3 have customers who book and then don't show up, so the question is why people leave. Flights 14 and 15 have no customers at all, so the question is whether they can even be booked. A no-show fix wouldn't help 14 and 15, and a listing fix wouldn't help 2 and 3.
